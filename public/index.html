How to Play

1. Start with A♠

Whoever has the Ace of Spades starts the game. Their first card must be A♠.

2. Follow the suit

The first card played determines the suit for the round. If you have that suit, you must play it.

3. No suit? CUT!

If you don't have the required suit, you must CUT. There is no pass.

4. CUT ends the round

When someone cuts, the round immediately ends. The highest card of the starting suit takes all cards played.

5. Win the round

If nobody cuts, the highest card of the starting suit wins. That player starts the next round with any card.

6. Win the game

After a round completely ends, the first player whose hand is empty wins.
